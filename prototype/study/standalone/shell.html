Help us test BridgeFinder

BridgeFinder is a CS 470 project by Evan Lin and Eric Liu. It helps UIUC students find researchers outside their usual circle, and shows who might be able to introduce them.

This takes about 20 to 25 minutes. You will:

  1. answer four short questions about yourself,
  2. try the app on your own,
  3. rate 13 to 15 suggested researchers,
  4. answer short questions about the app, and
  5. copy your answers and send them back to us.

We are testing the app, not you, so there are no wrong answers. Confusing parts are exactly what we want to find.

We collect no name or email. Your answers stay in this browser until you choose to send them. You can stop at any time with the link at the bottom of the page, which deletes your answers from this browser.
