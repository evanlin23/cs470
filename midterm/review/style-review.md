# Prose style review

The user requested a more natural academic style, including no colons, semicolons, em dashes or slogan-like closing lines in the authored prose. This is a style preference, not a scientifically validated authorship test. Editing these features cannot establish human authorship or guarantee a detector result. The report retains its AI-use disclosure and remains a draft for the authors to rewrite and verify.

## Research consulted

- Hans and colleagues, *Spotting LLMs With Binoculars: Zero-Shot Detection of Machine-Generated Text* (2024). Consulted the authors' [official repository README](https://github.com/ahans30/Binoculars), which links the paper and explicitly documents detector limitations and the need for human supervision. The method uses model-based scores, not a rule that a punctuation mark proves authorship.
- Liang and colleagues, *GPT detectors are biased against non-native English writers*, *Patterns* (2023). Consulted the abstract and results summary in the authors' [official repository](https://github.com/Weixin-Liang/ChatGPT-Detector-Bias). The reported false positives illustrate why apparent fluency or a detector label is not proof of authorship. These findings concern the tested detectors and samples, not every current system.

This was a targeted source check, not a systematic review or a fresh full-text review of every related paper. Neither source supports a universal punctuation checklist for AI authorship. No detector was run, and no writing was tuned against a detector score.

## Editing decisions

Removed colons and semicolons from the authored title, prose, metric labels and figure caption. Automatic figure/table label separators now use periods. There were no prose em dashes requiring removal. Proper names such as Adamic–Adar and numerical ranges retain their conventional punctuation. Reference titles and bibliographic metadata remain accurate, including punctuation in original titles, URLs and volume/page notation. Source-only paragraph-purpose comments remain for the authors' manual rewrite.

Replaced the rhetorical closing line with the concrete next use of the study findings. Rewrote metric fragments as full sentences and reduced abrupt contrast constructions where a direct explanation was clearer. Short factual sentences remain when useful. The purpose is readable academic prose, not the removal of every concise sentence or technical expression.

The section order, paragraph roles, rubric evidence, score formula, numerical results, participant facts, planned-study status and AI-use disclosure are preserved. No informal errors, invented anecdotes, artificial sentence variation or fabricated personal voice were introduced.

The report and Overleaf archive are rebuilt from the same source. Publication consistency, clean archive compilation and rendered-page checks verify the factual and layout consequences of the edit.
